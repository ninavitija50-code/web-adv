Negative things about technology

Technology has many negative effects on our lives and society. Although it makes our lives easier, using it too much can cause different problems. For example, spending too much time on phones and computers can damage our eyes, cause headaches, and affect our sleep. Technology can also make people addicted to social media and video games. Another negative effect is that people\ communicate less face-to-face because they spend more time on their devices. On the internet, people can also experience cyberbullying, fake news, and privacy problems. Furthermore, robots and artificial intelligence can replace some human jobs, leaving people unemployed. Technology can also make students lose concentration and spend less time studying. In conclusion, technology is very useful, but it also has many disadvantages. We should use it responsibly, spend less time on screens, and find a balance between technology and real life.
